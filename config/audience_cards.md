## Horizon definitions

Only definitions for horizons present in a pack are copied into it.
- BvA_Month: this month's actual versus this month's budget.
- BvA_QTD: quarter to date actual versus budget for the same months.
- BvA_YTD: year to date actual versus budget for the same months.
- RFvA_Month: this month's actual versus the rolling forecast.
- RFvA_QTD: quarter to date actual versus the rolling forecast.
- RFvA_YTD: year to date actual versus the rolling forecast.
- MoM: this month versus the prior month (balance sheet: versus the prior month end).
- QoQ: quarter to date versus the same months of the prior quarter (balance sheet: versus the same position last quarter).
- YoY: this month versus the same month last year.

## Audience: Management

Reader: business-unit leaders and the finance leadership team. Purpose: operational levers, cost drivers, next actions.
Style: direct and specific, 150 to 220 words of commentary. Lead with operating profit, then the largest material variances with drivers, then KPIs, cash and working capital.
Scope: business-unit detail and the management-only Adjusted Gross Margin are allowed. When you quote Adjusted Gross Margin, state the statutory gross margin beside it.
Layouts allowed: headline, variance, drivers, cash, questions. Use "questions" for open items an owner must answer.

## Audience: Board

Reader: the Board of Directors. Purpose: governance, strategic risk, quality of results.
Style: measured, 120 to 180 words. Lead with headline results, then risks, KPIs, cash and working capital, then the data-quality note.
Scope: use only the statutory results and comparisons listed in the Facts section; refer to nothing that is not listed there.
Required: include the data-quality note if the pack provides one.
Layouts allowed: headline, variance, drivers, cash.

## Audience: Investor

Reader: investors who receive budget reporting. Purpose: performance against the agreed budget and key KPIs.
Style: factual and neutral, 80 to 130 words. Report totals only, comparisons and KPIs exactly as listed in the Facts section.
Scope: refer to nothing that is not listed there. Name no customer, vendor or individual. Do not use the phrases "we expect" or "on track to".
Layouts allowed: headline, variance.
